CORE ANSWER · ANSWER FIRST

ICore Answer: Seven Layers Are Not a New School, but a System Integration

The 7-Layer Depth Model (Information → Material → Behavior → Body → Awareness → Subconscious → Mind) is not a new school of psychology; it is the layer-wise system integration of more than a century of the discipline. Behaviorism worked the behavioral layer; psychoanalysis deepened the subconscious; humanism looked up to the mental layer; cognitive therapy ran through information and behavior — each school drilled depth on one rung of the seven-layer ladder, and the model strings them into a single path to essence. When individuals, enterprises and the nation drill down along this ladder and calibrate decisions with the Law of Value Conservation — micro y = m × h × t, macro Y = E × S × T — multiplication takes over, division steps aside, a virtuous cycle of self-regulation is triggered, and win-win together with high-quality development follows.

MICRO · THE FORMULA

IIIMicro Proof: y = m × h × t — Why "Being Valuable" Multiplies

For the individual, total value = monetary value × happiness experience × time value. If any dimension hits zero, the whole account hits zero; when all three exceed one, life enters a compounding channel.

BOOK CASE
Man's Search for Meaning · Viktor Frankl

In the concentration camp, those who could find a "why to live" (Mental Layer) were more likely to survive — meaning keeps happiness and time value from zeroing out; this is the multiplication of h × t.

REAL CASE
High pay, the 996 grind, growing exhaustion

Monetary value m rises while happiness h and time t fall; the total shrinks — the division trap: money traded for the body (Bodily Layer) cannot buy back happiness.

REAL CASE
The parent–child mismatch

Parents reason at the information layer; the child needs to be seen at the awareness layer. Talk stuck on the surface sinks into the subconscious and becomes the next conflict.

BOOK CASE
Atomic Habits · James Clear

A 1% improvement each day (Behavioral Layer) compounds to about 37× in a year — habits are the compounding engine of time value; every cent saved is a life resource you can redeploy.

Earning= information-layer skills + behavioral-layer action: the art of efficiency and exchange.
Saving= material-layer restraint + behavioral-layer habits: respect for time value.
Being Valuable= subconscious-layer beliefs + mental-layer meaning: credit, competence, relationships — never reset by a single transaction.
Aha: earning is addition, saving is subtraction, being valuable is multiplication — the total compounds when you stand on the multiplication side.
MACRO · THE FORMULA

IVMacro Proof: Y = E × S × T — Win-Win and High-Quality Development

For nation and enterprise, total socioeconomic value = economic value × social value × time value. Micro virtuous cycles amplify layer by layer into macro win-win — the multiplicative foundation of high-quality development.

BOOK CASE
The Path · Kazuo Inamori

"As a human being, what is right?" — altruistic management: let employees be happy (S) first and stay long-term (T); economic value (E) keeps growing. Kyocera and KDDI showed that the multiplication formula lets an enterprise endure.

REAL CASE
The "false boom" of involution

Competing on overtime for short-term results (E) while sacrificing employee happiness (S) and health (T) — a locally rational division that buys talent flight and brand erosion.

REAL CASE
Countering involution & common prosperity

Governance at the national level is essentially installing a dashboard for S and T: easing the education burden, healthcare reform, labor protection — checking E from racing alone and calibrating the three dimensions.

REAL CASE
New quality productive forces

A mode of production advancing efficiency (E), social value (S) and sustainability (T) together — green, innovative, long-term — the policy expression of the macro multiplication formula.

Top-down connection: the virtuous cycles of countless y's compose the virtuous cycle of Y (y₁+y₂+…+yₙ). Individual happiness → family harmony → enterprise vitality → national prosperity: multiplication along one chain.
Win-win formula: let employees earn happily, users buy warmly, and society gain increments — aligned, the total value multiplies.
